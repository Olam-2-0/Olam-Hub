> **Harmonize Your Mind, Academics & Life.**  
> A minimalist, liquid-glass operating system designed for university students to balance academic workloads, master deep focus, and safeguard mental well-being.

---

## ✨ Features

* 📊 **Holistic Equilibrium:** Real-time concentric rings tracking your daily Academics, Focus, and Routines.
* 🧠 **Cognitive Workload:** Smart task matrix that calculates mental strain and filters assignments by intensity.
* ⏱️ **Deep Work Studio:** Integrated Pomodoro timers with a zero-dependency Web Audio synthesizer (Rain, 40Hz Binaural, Cosmic Void).
* 🌿 **Mental Wellness:** Built-in 4-7-8 guided breathwork, emotional state calibration, and a micro-journal.
* 🌙 **Circadian Sync:** Habit tracking synchronized with daily biological energy rhythms.
* 🎨 **Liquid Glass UI:** Interactive physics background with dual themes (Alabaster White & Antigravity Dark).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3 (Custom Liquid Glass Design System)
* **Logic & Audio:** Vanilla JavaScript, Native Web Audio API, HTML5 Canvas Physics
* **Backend:** Node.js (Lightweight, zero-dependency static server)
